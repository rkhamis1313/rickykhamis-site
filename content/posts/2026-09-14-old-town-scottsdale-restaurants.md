Old Town Scottsdale packs roughly 65 restaurants into about a square mile, which is either wonderful or paralyzing depending on how much time you have to decide. The useful way to choose here is not by star rating. It is by occasion, because the districts each do a different kind of night.

Here is how I break it down for people who ask, and I get asked a lot.

## If it is a special occasion

**[Cafe Monarch](https://www.opentable.com/neighborhood/n/phoenix/scottsdale/old-town-scottsdale-restaurants)** is the reservation to make. Prix fixe, courtyard seating, and a reputation that travels well beyond Arizona: TripAdvisor has ranked it among the top fine dining restaurants in the country, consistently number one in Scottsdale, and Yelp named it America's most romantic restaurant in 2026. Book well ahead. This is not a walk-in.

**FnB** is the other answer, and for a certain kind of diner it is the better one. Arizona-sourced, vegetable-forward, and genuinely of this place rather than imported from somewhere else. It also anchors the Arizona wine conversation in Old Town.

## If you want the room to be part of the meal

**The Mission** does modern Latin in a dark, atmospheric space off Brown Avenue, and it is the one people describe to friends afterward.

**Citizen Public House** is upscale comfort food with a serious cocktail program. Chopped salad, late kitchen, good for a group that cannot agree.

**Roaring Fork** is Southwestern, generous, and dependable, which sounds like faint praise and is not.

## If you are in Old Town for wine

Old Town has quietly become the tasting room district for Arizona wine. **LDV Winery, Aridus Wine Company, Carlson Creek Vineyards, Los Milics Vineyards** and **Salvatore Vineyards** all pour here, mostly clustered in and around the Arts District. Flights and small bites, walkable between them.

If you did not know Arizona made serious wine, this is the two-hour education. Pair it with the Thursday ArtWalk and you have the best night Old Town offers.

## If you want a patio on the water

The Waterfront district along the Arizona Canal has the patio seating, and on a good evening from October through April it is the most pleasant place to sit in the entire Valley. Fashion Square sits at the north end if you want to shop before or after.

## If you are eating before a night out

Eat in the Arts District or along the canal, then walk to the Entertainment District. Do not try to have a quiet dinner inside the Entertainment District on a weekend night. The room will win.

## A practical note on timing

Old Town runs on a season. From roughly October through April the patios are full, the reservations are hard, and spring training and the events calendar push it harder still. Through the summer, the same restaurants are quiet and the patios are empty for good reason.

That seasonality is the single most important thing about Old Town, and it matters far beyond dinner.

## The part where I am a mortgage lender again

I am going somewhere with the restaurant count, and it is not a stretch.

**Sixty five restaurants in a square mile is exactly what makes some Old Town buildings hard to finance.** Agency guidelines cap the share of a condominium project's total floor area that can be commercial rather than residential. A mixed use building with a full ground floor of restaurants and retail can exceed that cap, and when it does the project falls outside agency financing. The borrower is fine. The building is the problem, and nobody tells you until underwriting. See [Scottsdale Luxury Condo Financing](/blog/scottsdale-luxury-condo-project-approval/).

**The seasonality matters even more if you are buying to rent.** Old Town income concentrates into a handful of months around the winter season, spring training, the WM Phoenix Open and Barrett-Jackson. A lender is not underwriting your best six weeks. They are underwriting a twelve month average, and they discount projected nightly income severely or refuse to use it at all. That gap between what a property earns and what a lender will count is the whole game in this submarket. The restaurants are the reason the district works. They are also, structurally, one of the reasons the financing is particular. Both things are true.
