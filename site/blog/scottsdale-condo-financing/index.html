Scottsdale Condo Financing: Loan Options, Requirements & What Buyers Must Know

By Ricky Khamis · April 8, 2026 · 7 min read

Condos are a big part of the Scottsdale, Arizona market.

They can be a smart move for first-time buyers.

They can be a second home to play.

They can be an investment strategy in the right location.

But condo financing is not the same as financing a single-family home.

This is where buyers get surprised.

A condo can look perfect on paper and still create problems with the lender if the building does not meet condo approval guidelines. That can delay the closing or kill the deal.

After more than two decades helping buyers, investors, and Realtors, Ricky Khamis has seen condo deals go smoothly and condo deals fall apart.

The difference is almost always preparation.

If you want to buy a condo in Scottsdale, your financing plan has to match the property.

Here is what you need to know.

Why Condo Financing Is Harder Than a Single-Family Home

When you buy a condo, you are not just buying your unit.

You are buying into a project.

That means the lender also evaluates the condo complex itself. They look at risk factors that do not exist in a standard house.

This can include:

  • How many units are owner-occupied vs rentals
  • Whether the HOA has enough budget reserves
  • Whether there is active litigation
  • Whether one owner controls too many units
  • Whether there are delinquencies in HOA dues
  • Whether the project is FHA-approved (if using FHA financing)

In Scottsdale Arizona, some complexes are perfectly financeable, and some are not.

This is why you do not wait until the last minute to check the project.

Scottsdale Condo Loan Options That Buyers Use Most

There is no single best loan for condo buyers. The best loan depends on your credit, your down payment, and whether the condo project meets guidelines.

Here are the most common loan programs.

Conventional Condo Loans

Conventional financing is often the cleanest route for condo buyers in Scottsdale.

It may offer:

  • Competitive rates
  • Flexible terms
  • Lower mortgage insurance with strong credit and a down payment
  • Strong acceptance of competitive offers

But the condo project still has to meet conventional condo guidelines.

Many do. Some do not.

FHA Condo Loans

FHA loans can be a strong option for buyers who need more flexible underwriting.

But FHA has one major rule that matters:

The condo project often needs to be FHA-approved.

If the project is not FHA-approved, FHA may not be an option even if you personally qualify.

This is a common issue for first-time buyers in Scottsdale, Arizona, who assume FHA will work anywhere.

It will not.

VA Condo Loans

VA loans can also be used for condos, but the project may need to meet specific VA requirements.

For eligible buyers in Scottsdale, VA, financing can be a powerful option. But the condo project still matters.

Jumbo Condo Loans

In higher price points, condo buyers may need jumbo financing .

Scottsdale has luxury condos and high-value units where this comes up often.

Jumbo condo loans typically require:

  • Strong credit
  • More reserves
  • Lower debt ratios
  • Strong documentation

Jumbo condo underwriting can be strict. That is why planning matters early.

Warrantable vs Non-Warrantable Condos in Scottsdale

This is critical for condo buyers to understand.

Warrantable Condos:

  • Meet Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac guidelines
  • Qualify for conventional financing
  • Have adequate HOA reserves (typically 10%+)
  • No pending litigation against the HOA
  • Balanced owner-occupancy ratios
  • No single entity owns too many units

Non-Warrantable Condos:

  • Do not meet conventional guidelines
  • May require Non-QM or portfolio loans
  • Often have higher rates or down payment requirements
  • Common in newer developments or investor-heavy buildings
  • May have commercial space exceeding limits

If you are buying in Scottsdale, knowing whether a condo is warrantable can save weeks of frustration.

Ask about project status early. Do not assume.

The Condo Approval Process: What Lenders Review

Condo approval is where most surprises happen.

Here are some of the common project questions lenders ask:

  • Is the HOA financially healthy?
  • Is there enough insurance coverage?
  • Is there pending litigation?
  • Is the percentage of rentals too high?
  • Are there too many delinquent HOA dues?
  • Is the project complete, or still under construction?
  • Does one entity own too many units?

If a condo fails these checks, it can cause a decline even if the buyer has great credit and income.

This is why working with a mortgage strategist matters.

At KhamisGroup.com, this is part of the upfront planning.

Occupancy Matters: Primary, Second Home, or Investment

Condo financing in Scottsdale depends heavily on how the property will be used.

Primary Residence Condo

Usually, the easiest to finance with the best rates and lowest down payments.

Second Home Condo

Can be financeable but often requires:

  • Stronger documentation
  • Sometimes more down payment (10%+)
  • Distance requirements from primary residence

Investment Condo

Often requires:

  • Higher down payment (20–25%)
  • More reserves
  • Stricter guidelines
  • More scrutiny on rental ratios within the project

If you are an investor, you cannot treat every condo like a simple rental purchase.

Some buildings are investor-friendly. Some are not.

That is why it helps to run the address early, before you get deep into escrow.

📋 Scottsdale Condo Financing Checklist

Before you go under contract:

✅ Get fully pre-approved with a condo-aware lender

✅ Verify the condo project is warrantable or FHA-approved

✅ Check the owner-occupancy ratio

✅ Ask about pending HOA litigation

✅ Review HOA budget and reserves

✅ Confirm insurance coverage meets lender requirements

✅ Factor HOA dues into your debt-to-income ratio

✅ Avoid new credit or large purchases during escrow

Mortgage Tips for Scottsdale Condo Buyers

If you want to win and close clean, follow these tips.

Get Fully Pre-Approved First

Do not rely on a quick pre-qual.

A full pre-approval strengthens your offer and reduces surprises.

In Scottsdale, Arizona, that matters when you are competing.

Ask About the Condo Project Up Front

Before you fall in love with a unit, ask:

  • Is the complex warrantable?
  • Are there known financing restrictions?
  • What is the owner-occupancy ratio?
  • Any HOA litigation?

Your Realtor may not have all of this right away, but a good lender can help guide what to ask.

Keep Your File Clean During Escrow

Avoid:

  • New credit cards
  • New car payments
  • Large undocumented deposits
  • Job changes

Condo deals already have moving pieces. Do not add more.

Plan Around HOA Dues

HOA dues affect your debt-to-income ratio.

In some Scottsdale condos, HOA dues can be significant. Make sure your pre-approval includes them accurately.

Strategy for Realtors Selling Condos in Scottsdale

For Realtors in Scottsdale, condo transactions can be smooth when everyone is prepared.

Here is the real strategy:

  • Connect the buyer with a mortgage strategist early
  • Identify financing issues with the complex before the contract
  • Set expectations about HOA documents and timelines
  • Communicate fast when the lender requests project information

When Realtors do this, they close more condo deals with less drama.

Frequently Asked Questions: Scottsdale Condo Financing

Is it harder to get a mortgage for a condo in Scottsdale?

Yes. Condo financing requires both borrower approval and condo project approval. The building’s HOA finances, insurance, and rental ratios all affect loan eligibility.

What is a warrantable condo in Scottsdale?

A warrantable condo meets Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac guidelines for conventional financing. Non-warrantable condos may require alternative loan programs.

Can I use an FHA loan to buy a condo in Scottsdale?

Yes, but the condo project must be FHA-approved. Not all Scottsdale condos qualify for FHA financing.

What credit score do I need to finance a condo in Scottsdale?

Most conventional condo loans require a minimum credit score of 620. FHA may accept 580+. Jumbo condo loans typically require 680+.

How much down payment do I need for a condo in Scottsdale?

Down payment depends on the loan type and occupancy. Primary residence condos may require 3–5% down. Second home and investment condos typically require 10–25% down.

Why would a condo be non-warrantable in Scottsdale?

Common reasons include high rental ratios, pending litigation, insufficient HOA reserves, single-entity ownership of too many units, or incomplete construction.

Can I finance a condo as an investment property in Scottsdale?

Yes, but investment condo financing requires higher down payments, more reserves, and stricter project approval guidelines.

How long does it take to close on a condo in Scottsdale?

Condo closings typically take 25–35 days, depending on how quickly HOA documents can be obtained and reviewed.
