First-Time Home Buyer Programs in Scottsdale, Arizona: Your Complete Guide

By Ricky Khamis · March 31, 2026 · 7 min read

Buying your first home in Scottsdale, Arizona, is a big move.

It is exciting. It is also easy to make mistakes if you do not have the right mortgage strategy from day one.

A lot of first-time buyers think they need 20 percent down.

They think the process is harder than it really is.

They think they should wait until everything feels perfect.

That thinking keeps people on the sidelines.

The truth is simple.

There are strong first-time home buyer programs in Scottsdale that can help you buy sooner than you think. But you need to know which loan fits your goals, your income, and your timeline.

After more than two decades in the mortgage industry, Ricky Khamis has helped buyers, investors, and Realtors navigate all kinds of markets. And one thing stays the same:

First-time buyers do best when they start with a strategy, not guesswork.

Let’s break down what first-time home buyers in Scottsdale, Arizona, need to know.

What Counts as a First-Time Home Buyer?

A first-time home buyer is usually someone who has not owned a home in the last three years.

That surprises a lot of people.

So even if you owned a home before, you may still qualify as a first-time buyer depending on your situation.

This matters because some loan programs and down payment assistance options are built specifically for first-time buyers.

That is why it helps to talk through the details early with someone who understands how to structure the right plan.

At KhamisGroup.com, this is where clarity starts.

Common Loan Programs for First-Time Buyers in Scottsdale

There is no single best mortgage for every first-time buyer in Scottsdale.

The right program depends on your credit, income, down payment, debt, and long-term goals.

Here are the most common first-time home buyer loan programs available in Scottsdale, Arizona.

Conventional Loans

Conventional loans are one of the most popular choices for first-time buyers.

They can be a strong fit for buyers with:

  • Good credit (typically 620+)
  • Stable income
  • Moderate debt levels
  • Some money saved for the down payment and reserves

Many first-time buyers do not realize that conventional loans can allow as little as 3% down in some cases.

This is often a strong route for buyers who want flexible terms and competitive pricing.

FHA Loans

FHA loans are popular with first-time buyers because they are more forgiving in certain areas.

They may help buyers who have:

  • Lower credit scores (as low as 580)
  • Higher debt-to-income ratios
  • Less money available for down payment (as little as 3.5%)

For many buyers in Scottsdale, Arizona, FHA financing creates a path into homeownership sooner than expected.

VA Loans

If you are an eligible veteran or active-duty service member, a VA loan can be one of the strongest mortgage programs available.

Benefits may include:

  • No down payment in many cases
  • Competitive interest rates
  • Flexible qualifying standards
  • No monthly mortgage insurance

For qualified buyers in Scottsdale, this can be a major advantage.

Learn more about VA loans in Scottsdale, Arizona .

USDA Loans

If you are buying in certain areas outside central Scottsdale, you may qualify for a USDA loan.

These loans offer:

  • Zero down payment
  • Low mortgage insurance
  • Flexible credit requirements

USDA loans are income-based and property-location specific, but they can be a great option for the right buyer.

Down Payment Assistance Programs in Scottsdale, Arizona

Some first-time buyers in Scottsdale may qualify for down payment assistance programs that help reduce upfront cash needs.

Popular Arizona programs include:

  • Arizona Home Plus Program – Offers down payment and closing cost assistance
  • Pathway to Purchase – Helps buyers with down payment grants
  • Home in Five Advantage – Designed for moderate-income buyers
  • Federal Home Loan Bank Programs – Provides grants to eligible buyers

Not every buyer qualifies, and not every program is the right move. But they are worth reviewing as part of your strategy.

The key is not chasing every program.

The key is choosing the one that makes the most sense for your financial future.

How Much Do You Really Need for a Down Payment in Scottsdale?

This is one of the biggest myths in the mortgage world.

No, most first-time buyers in Scottsdale do not need 20 percent down.

Depending on the loan program, you may be able to buy with far less:

  • Conventional loans: 3% down
  • FHA loans: 3.5% down
  • VA loans: 0% down (for eligible buyers)
  • USDA loans: 0% down (for eligible properties)

Your required down payment depends on:

  • Loan type
  • Purchase price
  • Occupancy
  • Credit profile
  • Property type

The better question is not just how little you can put down.

The better question is:

What down payment strategy puts you in the strongest position without draining your cash?

That is where mortgage planning matters.

You want a payment you can comfortably afford.

You want reserves after closing.

You want flexibility.

At KhamisGroup.com, the goal is to build a mortgage plan that works after closing, too.

What First-Time Buyers in Scottsdale Should Do Before House Hunting

Too many buyers start looking at homes before they are financially prepared.

That creates stress and weakens negotiating power.

Before you shop, do these things first:

1. Review Your Credit

Your credit score affects:

  • Loan eligibility
  • Interest rate
  • Monthly payment
  • Mortgage insurance
  • Overall buying power

Do not guess where you stand.

Know the numbers.

If your credit needs work, start fixing it now. Learn how with our credit prep guide .

2. Understand Your Budget

Do not base your home search only on the maximum amount you can qualify for.

Focus on what payment fits your life.

That includes:

  • Principal and interest
  • Property taxes
  • Homeowners insurance
  • HOA dues (if applicable)
  • Maintenance and cash reserves

Common First-Time Buyer Mistakes

Avoid these, and you stay ahead:

  • Waiting too long because you think you need 20 percent down
  • Making major purchases before closing
  • Changing jobs during the loan process
  • Using all available cash for the down payment
  • Ignoring closing costs
  • Shopping for homes before talking to a mortgage expert
  • Working with someone who does not understand the Scottsdale market

Frequently Asked Questions: First-Time Home Buyers in Scottsdale

What programs are available for first-time home buyers in Scottsdale?

First-time buyers in Scottsdale can access FHA loans, VA loans, conventional loans with low down payments, USDA loans, and down payment assistance programs like Arizona Home Plus and Pathway to Purchase.

How much do I need for a down payment as a first-time buyer in Scottsdale?

Depending on the loan type, you may need as little as 3% down with a conventional loan or 3.5% with an FHA loan. VA and USDA loans may require zero down payment for eligible buyers.

Do I qualify as a first-time home buyer if I owned a home before?

Yes. In most programs, you are considered a first-time buyer if you have not owned a home in the last three years.

What credit score do I need to buy a home in Scottsdale?

FHA loans may accept scores as low as 580. Conventional loans typically require a 620 or higher. VA loans offer flexibility for qualified veterans.

Can I buy a home in Scottsdale with no money down?

Yes. VA loans and USDA loans may allow zero down payment for eligible buyers. Some down payment assistance programs can also help cover upfront costs.

How long does it take to close on a home in Scottsdale?

Most loans close in 21 to 30 days. Working with an experienced mortgage strategist like Ricky Khamis can help you close on time or faster.

Should I work with a local Scottsdale mortgage expert?

Yes. A local expert understands the Scottsdale real estate market, pricing trends, and what Realtors need to close deals smoothly.
