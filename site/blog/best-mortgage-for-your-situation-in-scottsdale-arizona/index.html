Best Mortgage for Your Situation in Scottsdale, Arizona: How to Choose

By Ricky Khamis · April 3, 2026 · 7 min read

If you are buying in Scottsdale, Arizona, one of the biggest questions you can ask is this:

What mortgage is best for my situation?

That is the right question.

Too many buyers ask a different one.

What is the lowest rate?

Rate matters. But rate alone does not build the right mortgage plan.

The right mortgage for your situation depends on your income, your down payment, your credit, the property type, and what you are trying to accomplish.

That could mean buying a primary home.

It could mean financing a second home.

It could mean structuring an investment property the smart way.

After more than 20 years in the mortgage business, Ricky Khamis has helped buyers, investors, and Realtors navigate every kind of financing scenario in Scottsdale, Arizona.

And one thing is always true:

The best mortgage is not the most popular one. It is the one that fits your strategy.

Let’s break it down.

Start With the Right Mortgage Questions

Before choosing a loan, you need clarity.

Ask yourself:

  • Is this a primary residence, second home, or investment property?
  • How much money do I want to put down?
  • Is my income straightforward or more complex?
  • Do I want the lowest payment now or the strongest long-term structure?
  • Am I buying at a conventional price point or a jumbo price point?
  • How important is flexibility after closing?

These questions shape the mortgage decision in Scottsdale.

At KhamisGroup.com, this is where the process starts. Not with a generic loan quote. With a strategy conversation.

Conventional Loans: A Strong Fit for Many Buyers in Scottsdale

For a lot of buyers in Scottsdale, a conventional loan is a strong option.

Conventional financing can work well if you have:

  • Good credit (typically 620+)
  • Stable income
  • A manageable debt load
  • A solid down payment strategy

This loan type is popular because it offers flexibility and competitive pricing.

It can be a strong fit for:

  • First-time buyers
  • Move-up buyers
  • Buyers with strong credit profiles
  • Some second home purchases

But conventional is not automatically best just because it is common.

You still need to look at payment, mortgage insurance, reserves, and overall structure.

FHA Loans: A Smart Option for Some Buyers

FHA loans can be a strong choice for buyers who need more flexibility.

This may include buyers with:

  • Lower credit scores (as low as 580)
  • Higher debt-to-income ratios
  • Less money available for down payment (as little as 3.5%)

For some buyers in Scottsdale, Arizona, FHA creates an entry point into the market sooner than expected.

That matters.

The right strategy is not always waiting until your profile is perfect.

Sometimes the right strategy is using the right loan program now and building from there.

VA Loans: One of the Strongest Loan Programs Available

If you are an eligible veteran or active-duty service member, a VA loan may be the best mortgage for your situation.

VA financing may offer:

  • No down payment in many cases
  • Competitive rates
  • No monthly mortgage insurance in many scenarios
  • Flexible guidelines

For qualified buyers in Scottsdale, this is one of the most powerful financing tools available.

The key is making sure it is structured correctly from the start.

Jumbo Loans: Critical for Higher Price Points in Scottsdale

Scottsdale is a high-value market.

That means many buyers need jumbo financing .

A jumbo loan is used when the loan amount exceeds conforming loan limits.

This often applies to luxury and move-up buyers in Scottsdale, Arizona.

Jumbo loans can be a strong fit if you have:

  • Strong credit (typically 680+)
  • Higher income
  • Solid reserves
  • A larger down payment in some cases

But jumbo financing is not just a bigger conventional loan.

It requires more planning.

Income documentation matters more.

Asset structure matters more.

Debt ratios matter more.

This is where experience matters.

Non-QM Loans: When Traditional Guidelines Do Not Fit

Not every buyer fits neatly into a traditional mortgage box.

That is where Non-QM loans can make sense.

These loan options may help:

  • Self-employed borrowers
  • Business owners
  • Buyers using bank statement income
  • Borrowers with more complex financial profiles
  • Investors needing flexible qualification options

In a market like Scottsdale, this matters more than people realize.

A lot of strong borrowers get overlooked by standard lending models.

That does not mean they are not qualified.

It means they need the right loan program.

Investment Property Loans: Strategy Matters for Investors

If you are buying an investment property in Scottsdale, the best mortgage depends on the property and your investing goals.

You may be looking at:

  • Conventional financing for rental property
  • DSCR loans
  • Short-term rental financing
  • Non-owner-occupied loan options

For investors, the mortgage decision should support:

  • Cash flow
  • Scalability
  • Asset protection
  • Long-term portfolio growth

This is not just about getting approved.

It is about making sure the financing supports the investment.

  • Traditional income documentation doesn’t fit
  • You need alternative qualification methods

✅ Choose DSCR if:

  • You’re buying an investment property
  • You want to qualify based on rental income
  • You’re building a rental portfolio

Real Buyer Scenarios: Which Loan Worked Best?

Scenario 1: First-Time Buyer in North Scottsdale

  • Credit score: 640
  • Down payment: 5%
  • Best loan: Conventional with 5% down
  • Why: Better long-term cost than FHA for this credit profile

Scenario 2: Veteran Buying in Old Town Scottsdale

  • Eligible for VA benefits
  • Down payment: Prefers to preserve cash
  • Best loan: VA loan with 0% down
  • Why: No down payment, no mortgage insurance, competitive rate

Scenario 3: Self-Employed Buyer in DC Ranch

  • Purchase price: $1.2M
  • Income: Bank statement only
  • Best loan: Non-QM jumbo loan
  • Why: Flexible qualification for self-employed income

Scenario 4: Real Estate Investor in South Scottsdale

  • Buying rental property
  • Wants to scale portfolio
  • Best loan: DSCR loan
  • Why: Qualifies based on property cash flow, not personal income

Common Mistakes Buyers Make When Choosing a Mortgage

Avoid these, and you stay ahead:

  • Choosing a loan based only on rate
  • Ignoring the total monthly payment
  • Putting too much cash down without keeping reserves
  • Using the wrong loan type for the property
  • Assuming the bank already has the best option
  • Applying before understanding documentation needs
  • Working with someone who does not understand the Scottsdale market

The right mortgage should make the purchase stronger, not harder.

Frequently Asked Questions: Choosing the Best Mortgage in Scottsdale

What is the best mortgage for buying in Scottsdale,e Arizona?

The best mortgage depends on your credit, income, down payment, and goals. Conventional, FHA, VA, and jumbo loans are all popular in Scottsdale. A mortgage strategist like Ricky Khamis can help you choose.

Should I get a conventional or an FHA loan in Scottsdale?

Conventional loans work well for buyers with good credit and stable income. FHA loans are better for buyers with lower credit scores or smaller down payments. Your situation determines the best fit.

What is a jumbo loan in Scottsdale?

A jumbo loan is used when the loan amount exceeds conforming loan limits. In Scottsdale’s high-value market, many buyers need jumbo financing for luxury and move-up purchases.

Can self-employed buyers get a mortgage in Scottsdale?

Yes. Self-employed buyers can use Non-QM loans or bank statement loans to qualify based on business income instead of traditional tax returns.

What mortgage should I use for an investment property in Scottsdale?

Investment property buyers can use conventional loans, DSCR loans, or Non-QM financing, depending on the property type and investment strategy.

How do I know which mortgage is right for me in Scottsdale?

Work with a local mortgage strategist who understands the Scottsdale market. Ricky Khamis helps buyers compare loan options and choose the best fit.

What is the difference between a jumbo loan and a conventional loan?

Conventional loans are for amounts within conforming limits. Jumbo loans exceed those limits and are used for higher-priced properties in Scottsdale.
