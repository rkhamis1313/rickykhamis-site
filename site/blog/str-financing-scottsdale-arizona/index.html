STR Financing Scottsdale: How to Finance Short-Term Rental Properties in Scottsdale, Arizona

By Ricky Khamis · March 11, 2026 · 5 min read

Short-Term Rental Properties in Scottsdale, Arizona

Short-term rentals have become one of the fastest-growing real estate investment strategies in the United States.

And few markets offer the same opportunity as Scottsdale, Arizona.

Tourism demand remains strong, major events run year-round, and vacation rental properties continue attracting investors from across the country.

But many investors quickly discover something important.

Financing a short-term rental property is different than financing a traditional home.

If you want to succeed in the Scottsdale Airbnb and vacation rental market, choosing the right loan strategy can make a major difference.

After more than two decades helping buyers and real estate investors secure mortgage financing, one thing has become very clear:

Investors who understand STR financing and DSCR loans can scale their portfolios significantly faster.

Let’s walk through how short-term rental financing works in Scottsdale, Arizona, and how investors structure deals in this market.

Why Scottsdale Is One of the Best Short-Term Rental Markets in the U.S.

Scottsdale is consistently ranked among the top vacation rental markets in the country.

Visitors travel to Scottsdale for:

• luxury golf resorts

• high-end restaurants and nightlife

• major sporting events

• corporate conferences

• winter tourism

Major demand drivers include:

• Barrett-Jackson Auto Auction

• WM Phoenix Open golf tournament

• Cactus League Spring Training

• luxury golf tourism

• Old Town Scottsdale nightlife

Because of this consistent demand, well-located properties can generate high short-term rental income year-round.

That combination of tourism, weather, and events has made Scottsdale one of the most attractive cities for Airbnb investors and vacation rental property owners.

But maximizing returns starts with the right financing strategy.

The Biggest Financing Challenge for STR Investors

Traditional mortgage loans were designed primarily for primary residences, not investment properties.

Because of this, many conventional lenders require:

• full personal income verification

• strict debt-to-income ratios

• multiple years of tax returns

• limits on financed properties

These requirements can slow down investors trying to scale rental portfolios.

Many real estate investors also write off expenses on their tax returns, which can reduce their qualifying income under traditional mortgage guidelines.

That’s why experienced investors often turn to a different type of loan:

DSCR loans are designed specifically for rental properties.

DSCR Loans for Short-Term Rental Financing in Scottsdale

One of the most common ways investors finance Airbnb and vacation rental properties today is through a DSCR loan.

DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio.

Instead of qualifying based on personal income, lenders evaluate whether the property itself produces enough rental income to cover the mortgage payment.

The DSCR calculation is simple:

Rental income ÷ housing payment

If the property generates enough income to support the loan, investors can qualify without traditional income verification.

For real estate investors, this can be extremely powerful.

Key Advantages of DSCR Loans

• qualification based on property income

• no traditional debt-to-income ratio requirements

• simplified documentation compared to conventional loans

• ability to finance multiple investment properties

• designed specifically for real estate investors

Many investors purchasing Scottsdale Airbnb properties rely on DSCR financing for this reason.

It allows them to focus on cash flow and investment performance, rather than personal income limitations.

How Lenders Evaluate Airbnb and Short-Term Rental Income

Short-term rentals operate differently from long-term rental properties.

Income can fluctuate depending on seasonality, tourism demand, and occupancy rates.

Because of this, lenders often use specialized tools to estimate projected rental income.

These may include:

• Airbnb income projections from STR data providers

• short-term rental market analysis reports

• occupancy rate and nightly rate estimates

• historical income if the property is already operating as a rental

Some DSCR lenders rely on short-term rental income reports from platforms like AirDNA or similar market data tools.

These reports estimate expected annual revenue based on comparable vacation rental properties in the area.

The goal is to determine whether the projected income supports the mortgage payment.

Working with a mortgage professional who understands Airbnb financing guidelines and DSCR underwriting can make a major difference during this process.

Down Payment Requirements for STR Investment Loans

Short-term rental investment properties usually require higher down payments than primary homes.

Most investors purchasing vacation rentals in Scottsdale should expect to put down:

20% to 25%

Exact requirements depend on several factors, including:

• credit score

• property location

• loan program

• projected rental income

• DSCR ratio

Even with higher down payments, financing allows investors to leverage capital instead of paying all cash.

That leverage is what allows many investors to build scalable real estate portfolios.

Choosing the Right Scottsdale STR Property

Financing matters, but the property itself is just as important.

Successful Scottsdale short-term rentals typically share several characteristics.

Strong Location

Properties close to entertainment, golf courses, and nightlife tend to generate stronger booking demand.

Popular short-term rental locations include:

• Old Town Scottsdale

• North Scottsdale

• Kierland area

• Scottsdale Quarter

These areas attract visitors year-round.

Layout That Appeals to Groups

Vacation rentals perform best when they accommodate multiple guests.

Homes with the following features often perform well:

• 3–5 bedrooms

• pools and outdoor entertaining areas

• open floor plans

• proximity to golf, resorts, and entertainment

These amenities help maximize occupancy and nightly rates.

Financing Multiple Short-Term Rental Properties

One of the biggest advantages of DSCR financing is the ability to scale.

Traditional lenders often limit how many properties an investor can finance.

Many DSCR loan programs allow investors to continue purchasing multiple income-producing properties.

This allows investors to:

• acquire multiple Airbnb properties

• expand vacation rental portfolios

• build long-term wealth through real estate

Many successful Scottsdale investors follow a similar strategy — purchasing one property at a time while steadily building a portfolio of short-term rental assets.

Frequently Asked Questions About STR Financing in Scottsdale

Can I qualify for a loan using Airbnb income?

Yes. Many DSCR lenders allow investors to qualify based on projected short-term rental income using third-party STR market reports.

What credit score is needed for a DSCR loan?

Most DSCR lenders require a minimum credit score of around 620–680, although stronger credit can lead to better loan terms.

Can I finance multiple Airbnb properties?

Yes. Many DSCR loan programs allow investors to finance multiple rental properties without traditional portfolio limits.

Can I close a DSCR loan in an LLC?

Many investment property lenders allow LLC ownership, which is common for real estate investors.
