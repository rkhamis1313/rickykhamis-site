Scottsdale Mortgage Payment Breakdown: What Your Monthly Payment Really Includes

By Ricky Khamis · May 6, 2026 · 6 min read

If you are buying a home in Scottsdale, Arizona, you need to understand one thing before you shop.

Your monthly payment is not just the loan amount and the rate.

It is a full monthly housing cost.

In Scottsdale, this matters even more because:

  • Property taxes vary by area
  • Insurance costs can be higher on certain homes
  • HOA dues are common in many communities
  • Condo financing often includes larger HOA budgets
  • Jumbo payments get sensitive to small rate changes

After more than two decades helping buyers, investors, and Realtors, Ricky Khamis has seen buyers fall in love with a price only to be surprised by the payment.

That is avoidable.

At KhamisGroup.com, we build the strategy around the payment first. That is how buyers win in Scottsdale, Arizona.

Let’s break down what your mortgage payment in Scottsdale really includes and how to plan correctly.

What Does PITI Mean for Scottsdale Buyers?

Most monthly mortgage payments include PITI plus extras.

P = Principal

The portion that reduces your loan balance each month. Builds equity over time.

I = Interest

What the lender charges for lending you the money. Based on your rate and loan balance.

T = Taxes

Your monthly property tax contribution is held in escrow and paid by the lender to the county.

I = Insurance

Your monthly homeowners insurance contribution is held in escrow and paid by the lender.

Plus:

  • PMI (if the down payment is under 20% on conventional)
  • HOA dues (if buying in a Scottsdale community or condo)

This is your total monthly housing cost. Not just principal and interest.

The 5 Parts of a Scottsdale Mortgage Payment

1. Principal: The Amount That Pays Down Your Loan

Principal is the portion of your payment that reduces your loan balance.

In the early years of most mortgages, the principal is a smaller portion of the payment. Interest is larger.

Over time, that shifts.

For buyers in Scottsdale, principal matters because it builds equity. But it should not be the only focus.

The goal is a payment that fits your life, not just a plan to pay down fast.

2. Interest: The Cost of Borrowing

Interest is what the lender earns for lending you the money.

It is based on:

  • Your interest rate
  • Your loan balance
  • Your loan term

In Scottsdale, Arizona, even a small change in rate can cause a major payment shift because purchase prices can be higher.

This is why rate strategy matters.

But here is the part most buyers miss.

The best mortgage strategy is not always the lowest rate.

Sometimes it is:

  • A better loan program fit
  • Lower total closing costs
  • A payment that stays comfortable without draining reserves

The rate is important. Structure is the full game.

3. Property Taxes: The Payment That Surprises Buyers

Property taxes are a major factor in Scottsdale’s monthly payments.

Taxes vary based on:

  • Assessed value
  • Location
  • Local tax rates
  • Special district assessments

Your lender will estimate taxes and include them in the monthly payment if you have an escrow account.

This means you are paying taxes monthly, not annually.

Buyers often look at a mortgage calculator and forget taxes.

Then the real payment shows up, and it is higher than expected.

This is why local planning matters in Scottsdale, Arizona.

4. Homeowners Insurance: Required and Often Underestimated

Your lender requires homeowners’ insurance.

The monthly portion of the insurance premium is included in your payment if escrowed.

Insurance costs vary based on:

  • Replacement cost
  • Deductibles
  • Roof age
  • Claims history
  • Location factors
  • Property features like pools or guest houses

In Scottsdale, higher-value homes and unique properties can increase insurance costs.

This is why insurance should be quoted early, not late.

5. Mortgage Insurance and HOA: The Add-Ons That Change the Number

PMI (Mortgage Insurance)

On conventional loans , PMI typically shows up when you put less than 20% down.

PMI cost depends on:

  • Credit score
  • Down payment
  • Loan amount
  • Program pricing

On FHA loans , mortgage insurance works differently and can be longer-lasting.

HOA Dues

Many communities in Scottsdale, Arizona, have HOA dues.

HOA dues are not part of PITI, but they are part of your monthly housing cost.

They can range from minimal to significant depending on:

  • Amenities
  • Gated access
  • Community maintenance
  • Condo services and reserves

In condos , HOA dues can be high and can impact:

  • Debt-to-income ratio
  • Loan approval
  • Monthly affordability

Buyers should treat HOA dues like a fixed monthly obligation. Because that is what they are.

How Buyers Should Shop in Scottsdale: Payment First

In Scottsdale, I want buyers to shop the right way.

Not by purchase price.

By payment.

Here is what to do:

  • Decide on a comfortable monthly housing cost
  • Work backward into the purchase price and down payment
  • Factor in taxes, insurance, HOA, and mortgage insurance
  • Choose the loan program that fits the plan

This approach protects buyers from stretching themselves too thin.

It also helps Realtors guide buyers more effectively.

Tips for Scottsdale Investors

Investors should look at the monthly payment through a cash flow lens.

You should evaluate:

  • Principal and interest
  • Taxes and insurance
  • HOA if any
  • Vacancy and maintenance assumptions
  • Property management costs if applicable

A mortgage payment that looks fine on paper can crush cash flow if the full expense picture is not included.

This is where investors win or lose in Scottsdale, Arizona.

Frequently Asked Questions: Scottsdale Mortgage Payment Breakdown

What is included in a Scottsdale mortgage payment?

A Scottsdale mortgage payment typically includes principal, interest, property taxes, homeowners’ insurance, and mortgage insurance if applicable. If you are buying in an HOA community, HOA dues are an additional monthly cost.

What is PITI in a mortgage payment?

PITI stands for Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance. These are the four core components of most mortgage payments. HOA dues and mortgage insurance may also be included, depending on your situation.

How much are property taxes in Scottsdale, Arizona?

Property taxes in Scottsdale vary by location and assessed value. Most buyers can estimate $4,000–$10,000+ annually, depending on the purchase price and area. Your lender will include the monthly portion in your payment estimate.

How do HOA dues affect my Scottsdale mortgage payment?

HOA dues are not part of your mortgage payment but are part of your total monthly housing cost. They also affect your debt-to-income ratio during underwriting, which can impact loan approval.

Does my mortgage payment include homeowners’ insurance in Scottsdale?

If you have an escrow account (which most lenders require), your monthly payment includes a portion of your annual homeowners’ insurance premium. The lender pays the insurance company when it is due.

What is PMI, and how does it affect my Scottsdale payment?

PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) is added to your payment when you put less than 20% down on a conventional loan. It typically costs 0.2–1.5% of the loan amount annually and can be removed once you reach 80% loan-to-value.

How do I calculate my full monthly housing cost in Scottsdale?

Add together your principal and interest payment, monthly property tax estimate, homeowners’ insurance monthly portion, PMI (if applicable), and HOA dues (if applicable). This gives you your total monthly housing cost.

Why is my mortgage payment higher than the online calculator showed?

Online calculators often show only principal and interest. The full payment includes taxes, insurance, PMI, and HOA dues, which can add hundreds to thousands of dollars to the base payment.
