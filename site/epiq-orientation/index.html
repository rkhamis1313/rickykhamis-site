What you get

Half a point toward your buyer

On a Realty ONE Group agent's transaction, a lender credit of half a percent of the loan amount. Your buyer chooses: put it toward closing costs, or use it to buy the rate down for the first year of the loan. On a $500,000 loan that is $2,500 of real money on your side of the table. Subject to program limits and credit caps, confirmed on the specific file.
